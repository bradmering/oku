# 06 — Migration from the legacy format

> Generated by running `npm test` against `fixtures/legacy/`. Those four documents predate the
> spec; **every failure below is either a migration we owe or a schema gap.** Re-run the validator
> after any schema change to refresh this list.

## What the drift report actually found

Only **two** distinct problems across three story documents. The format drifted less than the
absence of a version field suggested — but both problems are structural.

### 1. Cue fields live on the chapter, not inside `cue`

Every stage-driving chapter in all three stories carries its camera instruction as flat fields:

```yaml
# legacy
- type: article
  coordinates: [-144.6, 69.1]
  zoom: 12
  pitch: 50          # ← camera tilt
  bearing: 20
  routeProgress: 0.4
  marker: true
```

```yaml
# spec
- type: article
  cue:
    kind: position
    coordinates: [-144.6, 69.1]
    zoom: 12
    tilt: 50         # ← renamed
    bearing: 20
    routeProgress: 0.4
    marker: true
```

**Counts:** brooks-range 11 chapters · canning-river 7 · great-wheel 9.

This is the change that makes `decisions/0002` (thread + stage) real in the data rather than just
in prose, and it's what allows `decisions/0005` (a cue may be temporal) to exist at all — you
cannot have two cue kinds when the cue is spread across the chapter's own fields.

### 2. `pitch` means camera tilt in every legacy story

Confirmed by the drift report: `pitch` appears in the unrecognized-key list on **every**
stage-driving chapter in all three documents. Per `decisions/0007`, camera angle becomes `tilt`
and `pitch` is reserved for its climbing meaning.

**The rename is mechanical and safe** — it only appears in map/article chapters in these files,
never in the climbing sense, because none of the legacy stories are topo stories.

### 3. Envelope fields the legacy documents have never had

`specVersion` · `id` · `slug` · `dates` · `authors`

`slug` and `dates` are derivable (filename; the existing `date` field). `authors` needs a real
value — these were all single-author, so it's a one-element array. **`specVersion` is the point of
the exercise:** its absence is why the schema drifted three times without anyone noticing.

### 4. `hulahula-plan` is not a story

It has no `chapters` array at all. It's an `itinerary` of planned stops with cues, dates, and route
slices — **the fourth schema**, and the plan side of `04-formats.md`.

**It should not be force-fit into `Trip`.** The open question in `01-data-model.md` — whether
`planned` belongs on a Segment or a plan is its own Trip — has to be answered before this fixture
can pass. Leaving it failing is the honest state.

## Migration order

1. **Add `specVersion`, `id`, `slug`, `dates`, `authors`** to the three stories. Mechanical.
2. **Nest cue fields under `cue: { kind: position, … }`** and **rename `pitch` → `tilt`**. One
   codemod; the two changes touch the same fields so do them together.
3. **Update the renderers** to read `chapter.cue` instead of flat fields. This is the only step
   that touches app code.
4. **Decide the plan model**, then migrate `hulahula-plan`.

**Do not hand-edit the legacy fixtures to make them pass.** Their value is that they are real and
that they disagree with each other. Migrate them with a script that's committed, so the migration
itself is reviewable.
